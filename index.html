Berry Baron

A little mouse, a growing garden and an ancient map with a secret. Juicy match-3 puzzles meet a cozy story of friendship.

Your adventure

  • Swap neighboring fruit to complete every level goal before your moves run out. Tap a goal to see its instructions.
  • Make squares, crosses and longer matches for powerful combinations. Earn six kinds of helpers through play, with no real-money purchases.
  • Visit World to clear the garden, plant fruit trees and berry bushes, collect harvests and upgrade your barn.
  • Meet traveling merchants. Offers refresh every two hours and have limited uses per refresh.
  • Help story visitors, read their letters and collect map fragments. The Story Book lets you reread completed episodes.
  • Build an expedition over time to discover a snowy shore you can decorate.

Settings and progress

Language, music, sounds and vibration can be adjusted in Settings. Interface languages: English, Spanish, Brazilian Portuguese, Russian and Korean. New story dialogue is in English and Russian.

The game works offline. Your progress is saved locally on this device. Removing the app may remove your progress. Cross-device sync is not included.

No ads. No purchases. No account.

All gold and helpers are earned in the game. NPC trading uses virtual resources only. There is no player-to-player trading, account registration, advertising, analytics SDK or tracking.
