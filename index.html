MATCH 3 & TRADE

Berry Baron

A cozy fruit-filled world with 80 levels, four illustrated islands, a snowy winter journey and friendly traveling merchants. Free for iPhone, with no ads, purchases or account.

Support

Email developer.apps@pm.me with your iPhone model, iOS version and a short description of the issue. If useful, include a screenshot without personal information.

How to play

Swap neighboring fruits to match three or more. Match four for lightning, five for a rainbow, or a T, L or cross for a bomb that clears a 3×3 area. Complete every goal before your moves run out.

Ice appears from level 6. Chains appear from level 12 and keep fruits in place until matched or hit by a power-up. Thick ice appears from level 26 and takes two hits. Clear all ice and chains to win.

Trading with merchants

Every harvested fruit becomes a resource. In-game merchants exchange resources for other fruits or cosmetic looks. Offers rotate every six hours using device time. There is no real-money or player-to-player trading.

Languages

English, Español, Português (Brasil), Русский and 한국어. Choose your preferred language in Settings.

Your progress

Your progress stays on your device and you can play offline. Deleting the app may delete your progress. There is no cross-device sync.

Privacy

The game does not collect or transmit personal data. Read our Privacy Policy.
